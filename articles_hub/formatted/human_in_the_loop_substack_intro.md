# AI Music Evaluation: Why Human Listening Still Matters

**Subtitle:** How musicologists and sound engineers help evaluate AI-generated music beyond automated metrics.  
**Suggested Short URL / Slug:** `ai-music-evaluation-human-listening`  
**Tags:** `AI Music` · `Musicology` · `Audio Engineering` · `Generative AI` · `Music Technology`  

---

AI can generate convincing music, but evaluating that music requires more than a numerical score.

In this article, I examine how musicologists and sound engineers can contribute to AI audio evaluation through critical listening, prompt-to-audio alignment, cultural context, and structured annotation.

The central question is not whether AI can generate sound. It is whether we can reliably determine when that sound meets the musical, technical, and contextual requirements of a task.

I also outline a practical human-in-the-loop workflow for evaluating generated music and explain why reproducible human judgment remains important alongside automated metrics.
